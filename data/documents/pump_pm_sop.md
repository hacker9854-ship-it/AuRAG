# Centrifugal Pump Preventive Maintenance SOP
**Document ID:** DOC-SOP-001 | **Version:** 2.1 | **Effective Date:** 2025-01-05
**Applies to:** P-101, P-102 (Feed Pumps, Train A) — Unit 100, Feed Section

## 1. Purpose

This procedure defines the mandatory preventive maintenance (PM) tasks and
intervals for centrifugal feed pumps P-101 and P-102, to prevent bearing and
seal failures that lead to unplanned downtime in the Feed Section.

## 2. Scope

Covers routine lubrication, vibration monitoring, mechanical seal inspection,
and bearing condition checks. Corrective maintenance following a failure
event is documented separately in the maintenance/incident log, not in this
SOP.

## 3. Preventive Maintenance Tasks

| Task | Frequency | Responsible Role |
|---|---|---|
| Bearing lubrication (grease replenishment) | Quarterly | Maintenance Technician |
| Vibration reading (RMS velocity) | Monthly | Maintenance Technician |
| Mechanical seal visual inspection | Monthly | Maintenance Technician |
| Bearing temperature check | Weekly (operator rounds) | Operator |
| Full bearing condition assessment | Annually | Rotating Equipment Engineer |
| Mechanical seal replacement | Per OEM rated service life, or on leak detection | Maintenance Technician |

## 4. Alarm / Trip Thresholds

- Bearing housing temperature: alert at 75C, alarm at 85C
- Vibration (RMS velocity): alert at 4.5 mm/s, alarm at 7.0 mm/s
- Mechanical seal: any visible leak is treated as an immediate work-order trigger, not deferred to the next scheduled inspection

## 5. Consequence of Deferral

Skipping or delaying the quarterly lubrication service is the single most
common precursor to drive-end bearing wear on this pump family. If a
scheduled lubrication service is missed, it must be flagged as an overdue
work order, not silently rolled into the next quarter's schedule, since
grease degradation is cumulative and does not "catch up" on the next visit.
