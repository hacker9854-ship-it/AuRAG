# Maintenance & Incident Log — Rampur Processing Unit
**Document ID:** DOC-LOG-001 | **Log Period:** 2025-01 through 2026-01

This log records failure events and their associated corrective actions
across the plant, for reference by maintenance and reliability engineering.

---

## FE-001 — P-101 Drive-End Bearing Failure (2025-03-14)

**Symptom:** Excessive vibration (>7 mm/s RMS) and drive-end bearing housing
temperature rising above 85°C, noted during routine operator rounds.

**Investigation:** Rotating Equipment Engineer Ramesh Kumar traced the wear
pattern to insufficient lubrication. The quarterly lubrication service
scheduled for 2025-02-01 (WO-1002) had not been completed — it remained
open/overdue at the time of failure.

**Root Cause:** Drive-end bearing wear caused by a lubrication interval
lapse; grease was not replenished per the PM schedule (see DOC-SOP-001
Section 5, "Consequence of Deferral").

**Corrective Action:** WO-1001 — bearing replacement and re-greasing per OEM
spec, closed 2025-03-15.

---

## FE-002 — C-201 High Discharge Temperature Trip (2025-05-02)

**Symptom:** Compressor tripped unexpectedly on the high discharge
temperature interlock during a routine startup.

**Investigation:** Maintenance Supervisor Vikram Singh identified fouling in
the intercooler tubes as the cause of reduced heat transfer between stages.

**Root Cause:** Fouled intercooler tubes reduced heat transfer, allowing
second-stage discharge temperature to exceed the trip setpoint.

**Corrective Action:** WO-1003 — intercooler tube cleaning, high-temperature
trip reset, closed 2025-05-03.

---

## FE-003 — HX-401 Reduced Heat Recovery (2025-06-20)

**Symptom:** Feed outlet temperature deviated gradually from the design
curve over roughly six weeks, with a corresponding drop in heat recovery
efficiency.

**Root Cause:** Tube-side fouling from scale buildup, past the recommended
cleaning interval.

**Corrective Action:** WO-1005 — chemical cleaning of the tube bundle,
closed 2025-06-21.

---

## FE-004 — PSV-701 Premature Relief Event (2025-08-11)

**Symptom:** PSV-701 (mounted on V-301) lifted at approximately 90% of its
nameplate set pressure, causing a premature relief event during otherwise
normal operation.

**Investigation:** Safety Officer Deepak Rao found that the annual PSV
calibration scheduled for 2025-07-01 (WO-1007) had not been completed,
allowing spring fatigue and set-pressure drift to go undetected.

**Root Cause:** Relief valve spring fatigue combined with a missed
calibration cycle.

**Corrective Action:** WO-1006 — spring replacement and recalibration,
closed 2025-08-12.

---

## FE-005 — T-501 Tower Flooding (2025-10-05)

**Symptom:** Differential pressure across trays rose sharply above the
normal operating band; tower flooding observed.

**Investigation:** A post-upset internal inspection (WO-1009, closed
2025-09-15, performed by Vikram Singh) followed a prior process trip but
did not identify tray damage from the associated slug-flow event.

**Root Cause:** Tray damage from an upstream slug-flow event during the
prior upset, not caught during the post-upset inspection.

**Corrective Action:** WO-1008 — replacement of damaged trays (sections
12-15), closed 2025-10-06.

---

## FE-006 — P-102 Mechanical Seal Leak (2026-01-18)

**Symptom:** Visible mechanical seal leak detected during routine operator
rounds.

**Root Cause:** Mechanical seal degradation after exceeding its rated
service life without replacement (see DOC-SOP-001 Section 3).

**Corrective Action:** WO-1010 — mechanical seal replacement, closed
2026-01-19.
## FE-007 — P-101 incipient cavitation

**Date:** 2026-02-11  
**Equipment:** P-101  
**Symptom:** Broadband vibration rose while bearing temperature remained near normal during low tank level operation.  
**Root cause:** Suction starvation caused incipient cavitation; the low-level operating limit was not maintained.
